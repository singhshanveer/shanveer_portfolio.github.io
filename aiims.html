AIIMS Bathinda

AIIMS Bathinda, a distinguished healthcare institution under the Pradhan Mantri Swasthya Suraksha Yojana (PMSSY), is at the forefront of delivering exceptional medical care and research. Within the institute, the Department of Nephrology plays a vital role in providing advanced kidney care, combining medical excellence with compassionate service. The department is dedicated to improving patients’ quality of life through cutting-edge treatments, research, and preventive health initiatives.

Logo Design

Project Overview

For this project, I was commissioned to design the official logo for the Nephrology Department at AIIMS Bathinda. The objective was to create a visual identity that captures the department’s commitment to medical precision, patient care, and institutional integrity. The logo needed to embody trust, professionalism, and the essence of nephrology while aligning with AIIMS’s overarching identity as a leading national healthcare institution.

The Challenge

Designing a logo for a medical department demanded a thoughtful approach that balanced emotional resonance with professional rigor. The challenge lay in developing a design that conveyed scientific excellence and compassion simultaneously. It had to be symbolic yet simple, ensuring clarity and adaptability across multiple applications—from official documents and reports to signage and digital platforms—while maintaining consistency with the AIIMS brand values.

Design Approach

The design drew inspiration from nephrology’s core focus—the health and functionality of the kidneys. I integrated symbolic curves and organic shapes representing dual kidneys into a structured, circular form that evokes unity and care. The color palette incorporated shades of blue and maroon, reflecting trust, vitality, and medical authority. A clean, serif typeface was chosen to convey institutionality and professionalism. Together, these elements created a logo that feels both authoritative and empathetic, mirroring the department’s balance between advanced science and humane care.

Medical Symbolism

The logo is designed around a refined medical insignia that represents healthcare, healing, and kidney care. At the center, the emblem creatively integrates the universally recognized medical symbol with the shape of human kidneys, forming a balanced and meaningful identity. The flowing curves symbolize life, wellness, and continuous care for patients suffering from renal diseases.

Winged Healing Concept

The upper wing-like structures emerging from the central medical staff represent protection, recovery, and hope. Inspired by classical medical symbolism, these wings communicate compassion and the department’s commitment to advanced nephrology treatment and patient well-being.

Kidney-Inspired Structure

The symmetrical curved forms on both sides subtly resemble human kidneys, directly connecting the logo to the field of nephrology. Their elegant flow reflects vitality, organ health, and the department’s dedication to specialized kidney care and research.

Circular Institutional Seal

The circular badge structure gives the logo an official and institutional appearance, reinforcing trust, professionalism, and academic excellence associated with AIIMS Bathinda. The surrounding ring creates a sense of unity and continuity in healthcare services.

Gold & Neutral Color Palette

The combination of warm gold and soft neutral tones symbolizes excellence, care, dignity, and trust. Gold represents achievement, prestige, and innovation in medical science, while the lighter background adds clarity, calmness, and a patient-friendly visual identity.

Conceptual Meaning

The logo represents a harmonious blend of healing, medical excellence, and specialized kidney care. By integrating medical symbolism with kidney-inspired forms, the identity reflects the mission of the Department of Nephrology at AIIMS Bathinda — providing compassionate treatment, advanced research, and hope for healthier lives.

Color Palatte

The colour palette combines warm medical golds with soft ivory neutrals to create a visual identity that feels professional, compassionate, and institutionally premium. The balance of gold and graphite reflects trust, excellence, and modern healthcare innovation within the Department of Nephrology at AIIMS Bathinda.
