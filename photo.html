Through My Lens

Where composition meets the candid. As a designer, I am naturally drawn to balance, contrast, and visual rhythm—elements that heavily dictate how I look through a viewfinder. This gallery represents my love for visual storytelling outside the digital canvas. It is a curated archive of light, texture, and geometry captured in the wild. Here, every click is an experiment in perspective and an ongoing study of the visual world around us.
